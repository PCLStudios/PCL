# PCL (Project Chronos Lock) Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Developer: PCL Studios (github.com/PCLStudios, @pclglobal).

**What PCL is.** A voice assistant app for Android. It has no accounts and no PCL server. We do not collect your name, email or location.

**Microphone.** PCL uses the microphone only while its listening service runs (shown by a notification). Audio is processed in one of two ways:
- *Music-safe mic (optional download):* recognised on your phone, offline. Audio does not leave the device.
- *Android / Google speech recogniser:* used for whisper mode and the chatbot (and when the offline model isn't installed). Audio is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

**Chatbot.** When you use the chatbot, the text you speak is sent to the A.I. provider you chose (for example Groq, Google or OpenRouter) using your own API key. Their policies apply. Your key is stored only on your phone and is never included in backups.

**Stored on your phone.** Settings, names, theme, widgets, programs, notes and your API key stay in the app's private storage.

**Ads.** PCL shows ads through Google AdMob (a small banner and optional rewarded ads). AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to serve ads and measure them. In the EU/UK and other regions you are asked for consent first. You can change your choice any time via your phone's Google settings, and a 24-hour Power Pass hides banners. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Donations.** Donations are handled by Buy Me a Coffee or sent to the public crypto addresses in the About page. PCL does not process payments.

**Children.** PCL is not directed to children under 13.

**Contact.** Open an issue at github.com/PCLStudios or message @pclglobal on X.
