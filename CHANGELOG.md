# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Modes now default to **Mode 1** and **Mode 2** everywhere (Home, Programs, voice, replies). Say "mode 1", "mode one" or "mode won".
- **Android home-screen widgets:** Chronos Lock, Always On, Mode 1 and Mode 2 buttons.
- Fixed programs not opening apps (e.g. Spotify) when PCL was in the background: uses the "display over other apps" permission, or posts a tap-to-continue notification.
- 8 new program steps: navigate, dial, set alarm, notification, copy text, open camera, ringer mode, and "stop here" (skip the rest when a condition is met).
- Whisper mode removed from Programs (it is a listening preset, not an automation).

## 1.0.0
- First public release.
- Wake word “Chronos Lock” or “Chronos”, 80+ voice commands, whisper mode.
- Music-safe offline mic (Vosk) so music and videos keep playing.
- Renamable Combat Mode, Quantum Focus and chatbot.
- Programs with 16 step types, IF conditions, chance and templates.
- Material You theming, fonts, glass UI, editable Home widgets.
- Beginner guide, About page, in-app privacy policy.
- AdMob banner and optional rewarded Power Pass (24 hours of unlimited widgets and program steps).
